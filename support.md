# Support

## Help & FAQ

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: On your device, in the app's own database. YABA works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account to create.

**Q: Can I use YABA on more than one device?**  
A: Yes. Turn on Settings → iCloud Sync and choose a folder in your iCloud Drive. Your budget is then kept in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. The files live in your own iCloud Drive; we never see them.

**Q: Can I back up my data?**  
A: Yes. Use Settings → Export Backup to save a full backup file you can bring back later with Settings → Restore from Backup. Settings → Export as CSV gives you spreadsheets to read, but it is not a backup.

**Q: What if I lose my phone?**  
A: Your YABA data is included in your normal iCloud or Finder backup of the device, so it comes back when you restore a new phone. If you use iCloud Sync, your other devices still have your budget too. Exporting a backup file now and then is a good extra safeguard.

**Q: Does YABA work offline?**  
A: Yes, completely. YABA needs no internet connection. Sync simply catches up the next time iCloud Drive is available.

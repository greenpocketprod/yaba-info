# Support

## Help & FAQ

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: All your data is stored locally on your iPhone in the app's database. Nothing is sent to a server.

**Q: Can I back up my data?**  
A: Yes. Use Settings → Export to download your transactions, budget, and accounts as CSV files.

**Q: What if I lose my phone?**  
A: Your data is stored only on your device. Export your data regularly and keep backups safe.

**Q: Does YABA work offline?**  
A: Yes, completely. YABA requires no internet connection.

**Q: How do I contact you?**  
Email: support@example.com

## Report a Bug

If you find a bug or have a feature request, please email us at support@example.com with:
- What you were doing when the issue occurred
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened

We read all feedback and appreciate your help making YABA better.

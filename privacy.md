# Privacy Policy

**YABA: Yet Another Budgeting App**

YABA is designed with privacy as the foundation. Your financial data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What We Collect

Nothing. YABA does not collect, transmit, or store any of your financial data, personal information, or usage patterns on any server. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Data Storage

All budget data, transactions, accounts, and settings are stored locally on your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) in a SQLite database file. This data belongs entirely to you.

## iCloud Sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync, YABA saves copies of your budget to a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive so your other devices can read them. These files are stored by Apple under your Apple ID. We cannot see or access them. Sync is off unless you turn it on.

## Backups and Exports

Backup files and CSV exports are saved wherever you choose. What happens to them after that is up to you. Files you import are read on your device only.

## Third-Party Access

YABA does not share your data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services. Your data is yours alone.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Your use of YABA after any update constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

# Privacy Policy

**YABA: Yet Another Budgeting App**

YABA is designed with privacy as the foundation. Your financial data never leaves your device.

## What We Collect

Nothing. YABA does not collect, transmit, or store any of your financial data, personal information, or usage patterns on any server.

## Data Storage

All budget data, transactions, accounts, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone in a SQLite database file. This data belongs entirely to you and never syncs to the cloud or any external service.

## Third-Party Access

YABA does not share your data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services. Your data is yours alone.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Your use of YABA after any update constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
